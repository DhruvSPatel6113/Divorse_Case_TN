# Project Limitations & Methodological Constraints

This document explicitly identifies known data gaps, sampling constraints, and prohibited extrapolations in accordance with Section 12.G of the Master Specification.

---

### 1. Temporal Asynchrony
- **Census Reference Year:** The decennial census marital status data originates from the **2011 Census of India** (Table C-02), as the 2021 Census was delayed.
- **Judicial Time Horizon:** The official court time series covers **2018 to 2024**.
- **Constraint:** Cross-sectional associations between 2011 demographics and 2018–2024 court caseloads are exploratory and do not capture intra-decade demographic shifts.

---

### 2. Stock vs Flow Incommensurability
- **Demographic Stock:** A census count of people recorded as divorced or separated is a population stock (accumulated survivors of marital dissolution residing in the state).
- **Judicial Flow:** Court statistics represent annual incidence flows (petitions filed, disposed, or pending within a specific fiscal/calendar year).
- **Constraint:** These metrics must never be equated or added together as independent case tallies.

---

### 3. Category Scope Differentiation
- **Family Court Scope:** Cases handled by Family Courts under the *Family Courts Act, 1984* encompass a broad range of domestic proceedings, including:
  - Petitions for Dissolution of Marriage (Divorce)
  - Petitions for Restitution of Conjugal Rights (Section 9 HMA)
  - Judicial Separation (Section 10 HMA)
  - Guardianship and Child Custody matters (GWOP)
  - Maintenance claims (Section 24 / 25 HMA, Section 125 CrPC / Section 144 BNSS)
- **Civil Scope:** General subordinate judiciary statistics from NJDG include all civil suits, original petitions, and execution petitions.
- **Constraint:** Broad civil or family court counts must never be represented as exclusive divorce figures.

---

### 4. Ecological Fallacy Warning
- **Aggregation Level:** District-level statistical correlations between demographic disruption density and judicial caseloads cannot establish individual-level behavior.
- **Constraint:** High district caseload does not mean an individual couple in that district has a higher propensity to divorce.

---

### 5. Access Restrictions & Ethical Guardrails on Case Microdata
- **Access Status:** The public eCourts Services portal relies on CAPTCHA verification, and its terms of service prohibit automated web scraping.
- **Ethical Guardrail:** Automated circumvention of access controls is prohibited. Individual case outcome prediction is withheld to prevent pseudo-scientific legal advice.
